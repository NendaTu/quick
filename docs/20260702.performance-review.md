# Performance Analysis Document (July 2, 2026)

**Audit Date:** 2026-07-02
**Auditor:** Jules (Senior Software Architect)

## 1. System Behavioral Observations

### Data Acquisition Throughput
- **Observed:** Acquisition speed is significantly below the baseline required for 250-asset monitoring.
- **Impact:** Bottleneck blocks rapid strategy iteration and makes the "compounding target" harder to reach due to high friction in backtesting.
- **Root Cause:** Conservative rate limiting (~5-15 RPS) and sequential gap-filling.

### Strategy Execution Fidelity
- **Observed:** `SimulationEngine` correctly replicates exchange behaviors, including Maker/Taker fees and slippage based on order book depth.
- **Verification:** Test runs of `main.py` in `paper` mode showed entries filling as expected, with fees correctly deducted from `equity`.
- **Finding:** The "Aggressive Break-Even" (moving SL to halfway between Entry and TP1) is a strong feature for protecting the compounding goal.

### Compounding Goal Assessment
- **North Star:** 1 USDT to 100M USDT in 378 trades (5% ROE per trade).
- **Current Capability:**
    - The `scalper` strategy targets high frequency (10-20 trades/hour).
    - The `mustafa` sweep strategies target high quality (1-3 setups/asset/day).
- **Feasibility:** Achieving 5% net ROE *consistently* is the primary hurdle. Current strategies are optimized for 1-5% RRR-based targets. The high frequency (thousands of trades across 250 assets) means a lower win rate (e.g., 55-60%) with positive RRR can still achieve the goal, but the sheer volume makes data integrity and system latency critical.

## 4. Summary of Blockers
1.  **Critical Blocker:** Data Acquisition Speed. This must be resolved before strategy development can proceed at full speed.
2.  **High Blocker:** Strategy Discovery in Backtest. Hinders the ability to test institutional sweep strategies.
3.  **Normal Blocker:** Database Write Batching. While functional, it will become a bottleneck as trade frequency increases across 250 assets.
