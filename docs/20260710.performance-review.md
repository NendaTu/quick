## Signal-to-Trade Conversion Analysis

- **Logged Setup Setups**: setup events are successfully detected and recorded in `docs/temp/` metrics logs.
- **Pipeline Droppage Point**: The drop-off in live mode was traced entirely to the limit order timeout/chase omission:
  - Once a limit entry order was placed, it was never registered in the `pending_orders` queue.
  - Since it wasn't tracked, it was never checked for timeout cancellations.
  - The asset remained permanently locked under `pending_entries`, silently suppressing all subsequent entry setups for that asset.
- **Resolution Impact**: Registering limit entries inside the timeout queue guarantees that unfilled limits are canceled after `LIMIT_CHASE_TIMEOUT` (10s), instantly releasing the asset lock and maintaining consistent compounding frequency.
