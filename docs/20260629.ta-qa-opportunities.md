## 1. Missing Methods (New Implementation Opportunities)

### [OP-001] Order Book Imbalance Delta (OBID)
- **Concept:** Instead of a static snapshot of bid/ask volume, track the *change* in imbalance over 1s-5s windows.
- **Relevance:** High-frequency compounding requires catching moves before they happen. A surge in bid side "padding" often precedes an upward impulsive move.
- **Recommendation:** Implement `ta/indicators/book_delta.py` to track `(Imbalance_t - Imbalance_t-1)`.

### [OP-002] GARCH Volatility Modeling
- **Concept:** Use Generalized Autoregressive Conditional Heteroskedasticity to forecast future volatility.
- **Relevance:** The current bot uses ATR (lagging). GARCH can help predict "Volatility Contraction" vs "Expansion" more accurately, allowing the bot to tighten stops *before* a breakout.
- **Recommendation:** Integrate `statsmodels` or a lightweight GARCH implementation to adjust `RISK_PER_TRADE` dynamically.

### [OP-003] Cluster Volume Analysis (Footprint)
- **Concept:** Track volume transacted at specific price levels within a candle (not just total candle volume).
- **Relevance:** Identifying "Point of Control" (POC) within a 1m candle can reveal hidden institutional absorption or exhaustion.
- **Recommendation:** Add `ta/patterns/volume_profile.py` to track intra-candle volume distribution.

## 2. Misused Methods (Correction Opportunities)

### [OP-004] RSI Momentum Rider Logic
- **Current State:** Tightens stops when RSI is extreme (<15 or >80).
- **Issue:** Extreme RSI on a 1m chart often precedes a "blow-off top" or "capitulation bottom" where volatility is highest. Tightening stops here often leads to getting "wicked out" just before the actual reversal.
- **Recommendation:** Momentum riding should be based on ADX (trend strength) or Volume Influx, not just RSI overextension. RSI extremes are better used as "Take Profit" signals than "Tighten Stop" signals.

### [OP-005] BTC Confluence Calculation
- **Current State:** Uses `(Price_t / Price_t-1 - 1)`.
- **Issue:** This is highly sensitive to 1m noise. BTC can "flicker" 0.01% and flip the global confluence filter.
- **Recommendation:** Use a multi-timeframe BTC Trend Bias (e.g., is BTC above its 15m and 1h EMA?). This provides a stable "environment" flag rather than a jittery "gate."

## 3. Underutilized / Not Properly Incorporated

### [OP-006] Institutional Sessions (Time-of-Day)
- **Current State:** Sessions are identified but only used for `idm.py` and a simple `SESSION_MULTIPLIER` for risk.
- **Issue:** Market behavior (volatility, fill rates, mean reversion) changes drastically between the London/NY overlap and the Asian "zombie" session.
- **Recommendation:** Create "Session-Specific Logic Profiles." For example, NY session should prioritize "Breakout/Impulse" setups, while Asian session should prioritize "Mean Reversion/Liquidity Grabs."

### [OP-007] Market Structure (BOS/MSS)
- **Current State:** Detected but only provides a "bonus score."
- **Issue:** BOS/MSS is the most reliable indicator of institutional intent. It is currently drowned out by "Score Noise" from RSI/MACD.
- **Recommendation:** Promote BOS/MSS to a "Setup Anchor." A trade should *require* a structural shift (MSS) or continuation (BOS) as the primary trigger, with RSI/MACD acting only as secondary filters.

### [OP-008] Correlation Filter (Stat Arb)
- **Current State:** Calculated during `warm_up` and used to block entries.
- **Issue:** Correlation is dynamic. A 0.9 correlation during the London open might drop to 0.4 during a NY news event.
- **Recommendation:** Update asset correlations periodically (e.g., every 1 hour) instead of once at startup. Use this to perform "Relative Strength" trades (Buy the strongest asset in a correlated group, Short the weakest).
