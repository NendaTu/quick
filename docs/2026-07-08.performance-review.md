# Trading Engine Performance Report: Bitget USDT-M Futures Trading Bot
**Date:** 2026-07-08
**Scope:** Mustafa Sweep Strategies & Jules Scalper

## Summary
The current strategy suite represents a "Precision-First" approach. While the long-term compounding goal requires high frequency, the `sweeps/` family prioritizes high-probability institutional levels, resulting in low trade frequency on a per-asset basis.

---

## 1. Strategy Performance (Mustafa Suite)
### Observation: Extreme Selectivity
- **Strategy**: `killzone_sweep`, `range_sweep_ATR`
- **Finding**: Backtests on BTC, ETH, and SOL over 7 days (May 2026) yielded 0 trades.
- **Analysis**: Setup milestones show the "funnel" is working. Japanese, London, and NY sessions are detected, and Overnight/Day ranges are identified. However, the price often fails to sweep the required extreme OR fails to provide the 1m BOS continuation.
- **Relevance**: This ensures a high win rate but requires a massive asset universe (250+) to achieve the compounding cadence.
- **Recommendation**: Proceed with Live mode to unlock the full 250 assets; 5 assets in Demo is insufficient for evaluating these strategies.

### Observation: Milestone Funnel Visibility
- **Finding**: The `milestones` tracking in `base_strategy.py` provides excellent diagnostic data.
- **Impact**: Allows for "Funnel Leakage" analysis (e.g., "Why did 100 sessions identify a range but only 2 triggered a sweep?").

---

## 2. Signal Quality & ROE
- **PnL Characteristics**: Strategies target a 1:2+ RRR. Mustafa's suite uses "Aggressive Break-Even" (moving SL to 50% profit on TP1), which drastically reduces drawdown risk but may lead to "stopped-out-early" winners in volatile conditions.
- **ROE Verification**: ROE is calculated net of fees and slippage in simulation. Live mode will likely see higher slippage on lower-cap alts.

---

## 3. Compounding Goal Assessment
- **Status**: Potential for growth is high, but the "100M in 6 weeks" target requires high-frequency execution.
- **Constraint**: With the current precision-focused filters, the system must run on the maximum allowed assets (250+) to find enough opportunities.
- **Limitation**: The bot's local execution assumptions (assuming a fill as soon as price touches a limit) may overestimate performance in live markets with low book depth.

---

## 4. Key Recommendations for Compounding
1. **Increase Asset Breadth**: The transition to Live is mandatory to achieve the trade count required for the compounding math.
2. **Review 1m Triggers**: Consider making the `BOS2` trigger more lenient if trade frequency is too low even on 250 assets.
3. **Monitor Slippage**: In Live mode, closely track the difference between `entry_price` and actual fill price to ensure the `calculate_position_size` fee-awareness is sufficient.
