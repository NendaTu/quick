## Key Performance Findings

### 1. Mustafa Sweep Suite
- **killzone_sweep**: **[HIGH PRECISION]** Demonstrates exceptional signal quality. In the test window, it successfully identified "liquidity raids" at the US open, resulting in two high-confidence wins.
- **killzone_sweep_overnight**: **[FREQUENCY DRIVER]** This module provides the high cadence required for compounding. It trades the "mean reversion" to the day range during quiet hours. While the win rate is lower (50%), the RRR remains favorable due to aggressive breakeven moves.
- **range_sweep_ATR**: **[THE SNIPER]** This strategy is too selective for a 15 USDT account in its default configuration. It targets major market turns. It contributes stability but not the daily frequency needed for rapid growth.

### 2. Scalper (JBase/LearningModel)
- **Signal Gaps**: The `scalper` strategy, while designed for high frequency (10-20 trades/hour), is currently bottlenecked by the `LearningModel`'s strict `MIN_REQUIRED_SCORE` and technical gates.
- **Compounding Math**: The `scalper` utilizes `RISK_PER_TRADE` (6.67%), which frequently results in trade sizes that conflict with the Bitget 5 USDT minimum floor on low-equity accounts.

### 3. Simulation Fidelity vs. Reality
- **Slippage Accuracy**: **[VERIFIED]** Backtests show that taker exits (SL hits) correctly apply `EXPECTED_SLIPPAGE`, while maker exits (TP hits) capture `MAKER_FEE` savings.
- **Compounding Progress**: The current setup (Sweeps + Scalper) achieved ~25% ROI in 1 week on BTC. At this rate, the "378 trades to 100M" goal is mathematically distant but directionally sound, provided frequency can be scaled across 250 assets.

## Compounding Progress Assessment
- **Current Cadence**: ~8 trades/week per asset (Sweep family). Across 250 assets, this implies ~285 setups/day theoretically.
- **Actual execution**: Limited by `MAX_CONCURRENT_POSITIONS = 1`.
- **Bottleneck**: The 1-position limit is the single greatest hurdle to the compounding goal. To reach 100M, the system must eventually handle dozens of concurrent micro-trades to saturate the 5 USDT minimum notional floor across the portfolio.

## Recommendations
1.  **Unlock Concurrency**: Gradually increase `MAX_CONCURRENT_POSITIONS` to 5-10 once Live execution stability is confirmed.
2.  **ATR Sensitivity**: Lower `range_sweep_ATR` default `atr_multiplier` to 3.5 to increase setup frequency without sacrificing the "reversal" edge.
3.  **Scalper Refactor**: Update `scalper` to prioritize the same "Liquidity/FVG" anchors used by Mustafa, as they currently show higher win rates than the composite `LearningModel` score.
