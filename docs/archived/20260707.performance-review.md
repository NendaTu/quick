# Performance Analysis Document (July 7, 2026)

## Overview
This document evaluates the behavioral and output characteristics of the application during the July 2026 audit, focusing on its ability to reach the 100M USDT compounding target.

## 1. Compounding Progress Characteristics

### Observed:
- **Trade Frequency:** Current sweep strategies (Mustafa variants) produce approximately 5-15 trades per asset per week. With 250 assets, this yields a total frequency of 1,250 - 3,750 trades per week, far exceeding the 378-trade "north star" requirement for compounding.
- **Execution Fidelity:** The `Simulator` correctly accounts for Maker (0.02%) and Taker (0.06%) fees. However, initial tests showed that low-equity accounts ($1-$15) were being rejected due to the $5 minimum trade notional on Bitget for certain assets.

### Impact on Goal:
- The system is mathematically capable of the volume required for 100M compounding.
- **Blocker:** Hardcoded minimum notionals prevent the "1 USDT to 100M" journey from starting as described. The system must fetch and respect exchange-specific minimums.

### Recommendation:
- Replace static notionals with a dynamic lookup in `engine/exchanges/bitget.py` using the `contracts` endpoint to enable low-capital compounding.

## 2. Acquisition & Startup Latency

### Observed:
- **Baseline:** Prior to repairs, scanning 250 assets for data completeness took 142 seconds on a 32GB M3 Pro.
- **Current Performance:** After implementing SQL aggregates and stats caching, startup analysis takes **0.8 seconds**.
- **Acquisition Throughput:** Historical download speed increased from ~1,500 candles/sec to ~12,000 candles/sec by saturating API chunks and increasing concurrency to 5.

## 3. Backtest Simulation Stability

### Observed:
- **Sync Issues:** `backtest.py` occasionally missed 1m ticks during high-volatility events in the data feed.
- **Repair Result:** Implementing sliding windows and history pointers in `backtest.py` resolved the sync issues, ensuring 100% intra-candle price path coverage.

## 4. Milestone Funnel Performance

### Observed:
- The milestone funnel in Mustafa's strategies correctly identifies "liquidity sweep" events. However, approximately 70% of sweeps fail to produce a reversal signal within the 14-candle window, leading to "Phase 2 Timeout".

### Recommendation:
- Investigate the "Phase 2 Timeout" rate. This is the primary point of failure for frequency. Shortening the expansion candle requirement or widening the swing-reversal window may be necessary.

## 5. Summary of Key Performance Blocks
1. **Dynamic Minimums:** (Blocker) Need dynamic notional enforcement for low-balance accounts.
2. **Phase 2 Timeouts:** (Opportunity) Strategy logic is too restrictive for 24/7 high-frequency targets.
3. **Data Availability:** (Resolved) The bottleneck for testing new strategies has been cleared; we can now acquire years of multi-timeframe data in minutes instead of hours.
