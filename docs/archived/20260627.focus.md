## 7. RRR investigation & "Breakeven Trap" (Run 39)
- **Status**: Completed.
- **Findings**:
    - **The Breakeven Trap**: Identified a critical flaw where `BREAKEVEN_ROI_THRESHOLD` and `BREAKEVEN_PROFIT_BUFFER` were both set to 5%. This caused the Stop Loss to move to a price level that was essentially "ahead" of or equal to the current price, triggering an **instant exit** for a tiny gain (~0.03 USDT). This "illusion" of a win rate (85%+) masked a bleeding account.
    - **Noise-Induced Stops**: A 5% Net ROE target on 20x leverage forced a Stop Loss distance of ~0.15%. This is below the "noise floor" of a 5m timeframe, causing high stop-out frequency.
    - **Timeout Slippage**: Limit entry timeouts (10s) were performing market fills without slippage protection, leading to realized losses exceeding the risk budget.

## 8. Strategy Hardening & "Bi-Directional" RRR
- **Bi-Directional Recalibration**: Refactored `models.py` to ensure that if a Take Profit is capped by volatility (ATR) or floored by config, the Stop Loss is **re-calculated dynamically** to maintain a strict 1:2 Net USDT ratio.
- **Breakeven Decoupling**: Updated defaults to Trigger @ 20% ROI and move stop to 5% ROI buffer. This provides a ~0.75% price "cushion" to avoid instant stop-outs.
- **Timeout Protection**: Added `MAX_ENTRY_SLIPPAGE` enforcement to limit order timeouts. Entries are now cancelled if the price runs away during the 10s chase window.
- **Config Pitfalls**:
    - **DO NOT** set BE Trigger equal to BE Buffer; it creates a guaranteed exit.
    - **DO NOT** set Target ROE below 20% on small equity (15 USDT); fee drag and noise floor will compress the RRR to unprofitability.
