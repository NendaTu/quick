# Quantitative & Technical Analysis Opportunity Document - June 2026

## 1. Overview
This document catalogs missing, misused, and underutilized quantitative and technical analysis (QA/TA) methods relevant to achieving a high-frequency, high-win-rate compounding trading system.

---

## 2. Practical Opportunities (Direct Implementation Path)

### Missing: Variance-Adjusted Scoring (Kelly-ish Sizing) - [COMPLETED]
- **Concept:** Adjusting position size not just by ATR, but by the "Confidence Variance" of the scoring model.
- **Relevance:** In the quest for 100M from 1 USDT, capital preservation on low-confidence signals is as important as aggressive sizing on high-confidence ones.
- **Recommendation:** Implement a `KellyCriterion` module in `tools/trading_utils.py` that scales `RISK_PER_TRADE` based on the historical win rate of the specific `MIN_REQUIRED_SCORE` bracket.
- **Status:** **COMPLETED**. Implemented `calculate_kelly_size` and integrated into `models.py`.

### Misused: "Static" Indicator Periods for MTF - [COMPLETED]
- **Concept:** RSI and ATR currently use a fixed period (14) regardless of whether they are on 1m, 15m, or 1D.
- **Relevance:** Market dynamics differ across timeframes; 1m noise requires more smoothing than 1D trends.
- **Recommendation:** Refactor `ta/indicators/` to support "Adaptive Periods" that scale based on the `timeframe` argument (e.g., shorter periods for HTF to catch turns early, longer for LTF to filter noise).
- **Status:** **COMPLETED**. Refactored RSI and ATR with `TF_PERIODS` mapping.

### Underutilized: Imbalance Delta (Order Book Velocity) - [COMPLETED]
- **Concept:** `ta/indicators/book_delta.py` calculates imbalance change, but it's only a small weight in the score.
- **Relevance:** For 10-20 trades/hour, order book lead indicators (velocity of bids moving up vs asks) are often more predictive than lagging candle-based indicators.
- **Recommendation:** Increase the score weight for `imb_delta` and implement a "Lead/Lag" check: if price is flat but imbalance delta is spiking, front-run the candle close.
- **Status:** **COMPLETED**. Increased weight to 1.5 and implemented Lead/Lag logic in `models.predict`.

## 3. Theoretical Opportunities (Strategic Research)

### Missing: Regime-Based Risk Portfolios - [COMPLETED]
- **Concept:** The system treats all 250 assets under one global config.
- **Relevance:** High-beta altcoins behave differently than major pairs (BTC/ETH).
- **Recommendation:** Group assets into "Regime Buckets" (e.g., High-Vol Alts, Low-Vol Majors) with distinct `TARGET_NET_ROE` and `SL_MOVE` settings per bucket.
- **Status:** **COMPLETED**. Implemented `_classify_asset_regimes` in `Engine` and applied scaling in `models.py`.

### Missing: Statistical Arbitrage / Correlation Pairs - [COMPLETED]
- **Concept:** Trading the "Spread" between highly correlated assets when they diverge.
- **Relevance:** High-frequency compounding can benefit from "Market Neutral" strategies that don't rely on global direction.
- **Recommendation:** Implement a `CorrelationScanner` that identifies pairs (e.g., PEPE/DOGE) that have temporarily diverged from their 24h correlation mean, triggering a mean-reversion trade.
- **Status:** **COMPLETED**. Created `ta/patterns/spread.py` and implemented the Stat-Arb override in `Engine._asset_is_tradable`.

### Underutilized: Session Liquidity "Magnet" Model - [COMPLETED]
- **Concept:** Price tends to be drawn to previous session highs/lows before reversing (ICT "Power of 3").
- **Relevance:** High-frequency trades taken "in the middle of nowhere" have lower win rates than those targeting known liquidity pools.
- **Recommendation:** Implement a "Magnet Weight" in the scoring model. Signals that point *towards* an unswapped Session High/Low get a bonus; signals pointing *away* from them are penalized or blocked.
- **Status:** **COMPLETED**. Integrated session extreme weighting into `models.py`.
